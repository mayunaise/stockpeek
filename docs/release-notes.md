StockPeek 0.2.0 is the first public preview of a native Apple Silicon menu bar stock monitor.

Highlights:
- Compact ticker and hover watchlist, with stock/ETF details and minute charts.
- Up to 500 watchlist entries, multiple groups, drag sorting, and custom index carousels.
- Mainland, Beijing, Hong Kong, and US public market-data adapters.
- Persistent snapshots and session-aware polling.

Download `StockPeek-0.2.0-macOS-arm64.dmg`, open the disk image, and drag StockPeek to Applications. Requires Apple Silicon and macOS 26 or later.

This preview is ad-hoc signed and not notarized. The interface is currently in Simplified Chinese. Market feeds may be delayed or unavailable; holidays and half-day sessions are not yet modeled. Existing development-build settings remain compatible.

The accompanying SHA-256 file can be used to verify the DMG download.
